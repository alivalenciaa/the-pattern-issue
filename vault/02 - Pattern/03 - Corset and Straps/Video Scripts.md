## Video 10 — Corset Hole Row (First Side) 🎙️ VOICEOVER

🎯 Pinpoints: stitch count → "24 stitches along the edge" · SC group → "5 SC"

"Now we'll create the corset back. This is what makes the bra adjustable with lacing. We work this on the SHORT edge of the bra — not the top or bottom, the side. This row creates evenly spaced HOLES for threading ribbon or cord. The structure stays the same for everyone, but the exact spacing depends on your stitch count.

My example with 24 stitches: Chain 1, turn. SC in the first stitch. Ch 3, skip 3 stitches, SC in the next stitch. Work 5 SC. Ch 3, skip 3, SC in next stitch. Work 5 SC. Ch 3, skip 3, SC in next stitch. SC in the last stitch. This gives me 3 evenly spaced holes.

If your stitch count is different: keep the structure the same, distribute the SC groups evenly, you may have 1-3 stitches left at the end — just work 1 SC into each. Don't force the spacing to match mine. The holes should look evenly spaced and the row should lie flat."

On-screen: "Worked on SHORT edge of bra" · "Structure: SC, ch-3 hole, SC group, repeat" · "Designer's example: 24 stitches → 3 holes" · "Your spacing will adjust based on your count"

## Video 11 — Corset Reinforcement Row (First Side) 🎙️ VOICEOVER

🎯 Pinpoint: remove "continue to V-neckline" option → just fasten off

"Now we reinforce the holes so they don't stretch or distort during wear. Chain 1, turn. Work 1 SC into each SC from the previous row. Work 3 SC into each chain-3 space. Continue across the row. The result is a sturdy, reinforced edge with clean, even lacing holes.

Fasten off when you finish this row. Next, we'll move to the other short edge and work the same two corset rows there. After both sides are done, we'll come back and do the V-neckline edging."

On-screen: "SC into each SC" · "3 SC into each ch-3 space" · "Fasten off after this row" · "Next: corset rows on the other side"

## Video 12 — Corset Rows on Second Side 🎙️ VOICEOVER

🎯 Pinpoints: match new flow — both corset sides done before V-neckline

"Now we'll work the same two corset rows on the OTHER short edge of the bra. Reattach your yarn at one end of the other short edge.

Hole Row: same as the first side — Ch 1, SC in first stitch, then alternate ch-3 holes with small SC groups across. For me, with 24 stitches: SC, ch-3 skip 3, SC, 5 SC, ch-3 skip 3, SC, 5 SC, ch-3 skip 3, SC, SC in last stitch.

Reinforcement Row: Ch 1, turn. SC into each SC, 3 SC into each ch-3 space. Fasten off when done. Now both corset sides are complete. Next, we'll do the V-neckline edging."

On-screen: "Repeat corset rows on second side" · "Match any extra straight rows for symmetry" · "Fasten off after second reinforcement row" · "Next: V-neckline edging"

## Video 14 — Strap Placement 🟢 KEEP

"Now we'll attach the straps. These are fully adjustable and wrap around your body, threading through the corset for a custom fit. First, we need to decide WHERE the straps will sit on the top edge of the bra. Hold the bra against your chest. Decide where you want each strap to attach.

For lift and a push-up effect: place straps slightly inward from your natural shoulder line — closer together. For more comfort: align with where a normal bra strap would sit — farther apart.

Place a stitch marker at your first attachment point. Then fold the bra in half vertically to find the center, and place a second stitch marker on the opposite side at the same relative position. This ensures symmetry."

On-screen: "Strap placement affects lift" · "Closer together = push-up" · "Farther apart = comfort" · "Fold bra in half to find center for symmetry"

## Video 15 — Strap Construction 🎙️ VOICEOVER

🎯 Pinpoints: "150 chains" / "280 chains" → "500 chains" · remove uncertainty, be confident from the start

"Now we'll make the first strap. Hook is still 2.5mm. Remove your first stitch marker, insert hook into that stitch, and attach yarn securely. You can tie it twice for extra security.

The strap needs to be long enough to travel from the front of the bra, thread through the corset holes in the back, come back to the front, cross over the chest, wrap around to the back again, and tie securely with extra length to adjust.

My final strap length was 500 chains per strap. Step 1: Chain a long foundation. I chained about 500 chains. Step 2: Once you've chained your length, slip stitch back into each chain, working back toward the bra. This reinforces the strap and gives it a clean finish. Step 3: Fasten off when you reach the bra.

Slip stitching slightly shortens the strap, so you'll try it on after to check length. If it's too short, you can add more chains at the tip and slip stitch back down them."

On-screen: "Hook: still 2.5mm" · "Designer: 500 chains per strap" · "Step 1: Chain long foundation (~500)" · "Step 2: Slip stitch back along chain" · "Step 3: Fasten off at bra"

## Video 16 — Trying On & Adjusting Straps 🟢 KEEP

"Now let's try the strap on. But first — if you're using sequin yarn — you need to remove sequins from the strap so it can glide through the corset holes smoothly. I'll show that in the next video. For now, let's talk about adjusting length.

Thread the strap through the corset holes, wrap it around your body, and check if there's enough length to tie comfortably with extra to spare.

If your strap is too short: reattach yarn at the tip of the strap (the end farthest from the bra), add more chains, slip stitch back down the new chains, fasten off again. This way you can add length without redoing the whole strap.

Now make the second strap exactly the same way — attach at the second stitch marker, chain the same length, slip stitch back, fasten off."

On-screen: "Thread strap through corset holes to test" · "If too short: reattach at tip, add chains, slip stitch back" · "Make second strap matching the first"

## Video 17 — Sequin Removal 🟢 KEEP

"If you're using regular yarn, you can skip this video. If you're using sequin yarn like me, you NEED to remove sequins from certain areas before threading the straps.

Where to remove sequins: most or all of the strap length (so the strap slides smoothly through the corset), and around the corset hole areas on both sides of the bra.

How to remove sequins: the sequins are attached to the yarn — they can't be slid off. You have to carefully cut them off. Using small, sharp scissors, cut through the center of each sequin. Be extremely careful NOT to cut the yarn itself — only cut the sequin. The yarn stays intact.

Take your time with this. Cutting the yarn by accident will weaken or break the strap. It's tedious but worth it for a smooth finish."

On-screen: "Skip if using non-sequin yarn" · "Remove sequins from: most of strap + around corset holes" · "CUT carefully through center of sequin" · "Do NOT cut the yarn itself"
