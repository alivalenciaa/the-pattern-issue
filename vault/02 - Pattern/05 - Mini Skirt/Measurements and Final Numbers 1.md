# Miniskirt — Measurements and Final Numbers

Hook: 2.5 mm (throughout — foundation chain, Round 1 HDC, all LDC rounds)

- Mesh front (flat): ~14.5"
- Back foundation chain: ~14.5"
- Total where skirt sits: ~29" (lower waist/upper hip, NOT true waist)
- Hip target: 35" → stop increasing at ~17.5" across (but trust the try-on over the tape)
- Increase rounds: ~20
- Straight rounds: worked to desired length

> [!info] These are the designer's own example numbers, not sizing rules. See [[01 - Master Pattern]] — Core Design Principles.
