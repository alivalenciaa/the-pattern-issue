# Mesh Midsection — Measurements and Final Numbers

Hook: 3 mm (entire section, including coverage rows)

- Coverage rows: 72 HDC, 2 rows (1 straight + 1 decrease)
- Decrease sets: 1
- Straight sets: 6
- Increase sets: 8
- Total mesh rows: 32

> [!info] These are the designer's own example numbers, not sizing rules. See [[01 - Master Pattern]] — Core Design Principles.

> [!tip] Editing note (from Video 20): the designer originally worked 2 decrease sets, then unraveled back to 1 after trying it on — the back panel was short enough that 1 was enough. A longer back panel may need 2 or more. See [[08 - Revision Log]].
