## Video 37 — Ruffle Border (Slit + Bottom Edge)

**Part A — Starting the ruffle (bottom edge):**

"Okay — I've reached my floor length, my rows are done, and now we're at the very last step: the ruffle border. Once this is done, the dress is finished!

The ruffle goes all the way around as one continuous circle — along the whole bottom edge, up one side of the slit, and back down the other side. It's just one row. And we don't need a foundation row — we work straight into the chain spaces we already have.

To start: chain 3 and turn your work. Then into each chain space along the bottom edge, work 7 treble crochets. All those stitches packed into one space is what forces the fabric to bunch up and ruffle. [demonstrate one cluster slowly, then a few more, then time-lapse]

Keep going all the way along the bottom edge like that."

On-screen: "Last step: the ruffle border!" · "One row, all the way around — bottom edge → up one slit side → down the other" · "No foundation row — work into the chain spaces" · "Ch 3, turn" · "7 treble crochets into each chain space" · "Hook: 3mm"

**Part B — 🎙️ Voiceover: starting up the slit side**

🎙️ "Now I'm heading up the side of the slit. The holes up here aren't all the same size — some are bigger, some are smaller — so I'm just adding more stitches in the bigger holes and fewer in the smaller ones. You don't have to count exactly. Just fill each hole enough that it bunches up and ruffles."

On-screen: "Slit side: holes aren't all the same size" · "Bigger hole = more stitches · Smaller hole = fewer" · "Don't count exactly — just fill it enough to ruffle"

**Part C — 🎙️ Voiceover: the tighter part near the top of the slit**

🎙️ "You can see these holes up here are smaller, so I'm putting fewer stitches in them. Same idea — just fill each one until it ruffles."

On-screen: "Smaller holes up here → fewer stitches" · "Same idea — fill until it ruffles"

**Part D — Finishing + closing the dress:**

"Okay, my ruffle is done all the way around. Like I said, I just adjusted as I went — for the bottom edge I did 7 treble crochets in each chain space, and going up the slit I did around 10 in the biggest holes and about 6 or 7 in the smaller ones. But honestly, don't overthink it. Just fill each hole enough that it ruffles nicely.

Now to finish, all you do is slip stitch into the top of that very first treble crochet you made when you started the ruffle. [show it] Then fasten off and weave in your ends.

[cut to finished dress on the mannequin]

That's it — your dress is done! Go try it on. I really hope you loved how yours came out. If you make this dress, please tag me, I would love to see it. Thank you so much for following along."

On-screen: "Designer's counts: 7 TR in bottom chain spaces · ~10 in the biggest slit holes · ~6–7 in the smaller ones" · "Don't overthink it — just fill each hole until it ruffles" · "Finish: slip stitch into the top of your first treble crochet" · "Fasten off + weave in ends" · "[mannequin reveal]" · "YOUR DRESS IS DONE! 🎀" · "Tag me — I'd love to see yours!"
