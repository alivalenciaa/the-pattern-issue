The Mission

Crochet should feel like fashion.

The Pattern Issue is a modern crochet fashion platform for people who want handmade garments to feel elevated, wearable, and culturally alive.

Issue 01

The first release starts where the demand already is.

Maria's sequin crochet dress became the piece people kept saving, sharing, and asking about. Phase 1 keeps the website focused: one strong pattern, a premium product experience, and a foundation for the editorial world to come.

Long term, The Pattern Issue can grow into styling notes, tutorials, color stories, trend reports, and future crochet fashion patterns. Today, it starts with the dress.
